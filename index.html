The Triggers

Pre-decided · observable · specific

The playbook only works if it's pre-decided.

For each of the three signals below, write a specific, observable trigger value — and the first action you'll take when it fires. Not "when things feel tight." Specific. Already decided.

02

Safe-Cut Menu

Every non-revenue expense · next 6 months

You're not cutting anything yet — you're knowing what's available.

List every non-revenue-generating expense over the next six months. Classify each as momentum-preserving or momentum-damaging.

◆ Momentum-Preserving

Defers or eliminates things that don't directly support selling or delivering. Nice-to-have software, a hire that could wait, a vendor renegotiation.

Rule: you can pick it back up without rebuilding.

▲ Momentum-Damaging

Touches your ability to find, close, or deliver to clients. The ramping salesperson. The key account manager. Marketing that is working.

Rule: undoing the cut requires a new hire or new spend.
